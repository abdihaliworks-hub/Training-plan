The diagnosis

Why the baseline stopped moving

You reset every five weeks and re-peak to the same numbers. That re-expresses fitness you already have, mostly neural, instead of building the slow structural kind. Peak power comes back within two to three weeks. Capacity takes months. You have been harvesting the fast quality and never accumulating the slow one.

The fix: keep the five-week loop you like, but nest three of them. Each new cycle's volume weeks start from the previous cycle's achieved numbers, not from zero. Week 5 becomes a test week rather than a full taper. The shape stays identical; the floor climbs.

Honest caveats
  • Periodization for climbing is barely researched. This structure is a reasoned synthesis, not a validated protocol.
  • Seven months post-op and pain-free, your hand is not the limiter — inconsistency is. But if pain returns under load, that outranks the plan.
  • Collagen evidence is promising, not settled, and only works alongside loading.
  • Finger-extensor bands have weak evidence for injury prevention. Cheap, so included; do not over-trust them.
  • Abrahangs are real but over-hyped. Do not let them crowd out real strength work once you are cleared.
  • Wrist HRV is noisy. Use weekly trends, never single days.
  • Persistent fatigue, illness, low mood or stalled progress despite good training means see a sports physician. Low energy availability is the usual culprit.
